About This Course

GNS 201: Communication in English is a comprehensive course designed to develop professional communication skills in Nigerian students. This course covers the fundamental principles of effective communication across different contexts, from formal business correspondence to creative writing for publication.

The four-chapter course systematically builds your understanding from specialized vocabulary in different professions, through business letter writing, to advanced skills in article writing and report preparation. Each chapter includes real-world examples, practical exercises, and examination-style questions to ensure your success.
