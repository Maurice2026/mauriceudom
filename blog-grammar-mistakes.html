20 Common Grammar Mistakes Students Make

Master these essential corrections and instantly improve your academic writing.

Grammar mistakes undermine credibility. Whether in essays, emails, or formal writing, incorrect grammar signals carelessness to your reader. The good news: the most common mistakes are easily fixed once you understand them.

1. Subject-Verb Agreement

The error: The subject and verb do not match in number.

❌ The team are playing well. (Incorrect)
✅ The team is playing well. (Correct)

2. Their vs There vs They're

The error: Confusing homophones that sound identical but have different meanings.

❌ There going to there house. (Incorrect)
✅ They're going to their house. (Correct)

3. Its vs It's

The error: Using the possessive form when you mean the contraction, or vice versa.

❌ It's tail is long. (Incorrect)
✅ Its tail is long. (Correct)
