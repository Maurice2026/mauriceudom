What You'll Learn

Literary Appreciation

Understand the distinctive features of major literary genres. Master essential literary terminology. Analyse how literary elements create meaning. Develop ability to explicate and interpret literary texts with sophistication.

Essay Writing

Write compelling literary analyses grounded in textual evidence. Construct persuasive arguments about literary meaning. Develop skills in reviewing and criticising literary works across genres.

Oral Communication

Plan and deliver effective presentations for diverse audiences. Master vocal delivery techniques and physical communication. Build confidence speaking in multiple contexts.

Advanced Applications

Prepare professional radio broadcasts. Understand debate fundamentals and argumentation techniques. Perform literary texts with interpretation and expression.
